In high school, I became a very big fan of _Jeopardy!_, to the point that I once made my family go see Alex Trebek host the show while we were on vacation in LA. In my adult life, my love of quiz competition has taken a more active role in the form of Sporcle bar trivia. My friends at the UofM Biostation got me into bar trivia for the first time at a pub in Pellston (which was actually inside of the airport), and we had so much fun that we kept our team going back in Ann Arbor. When I came to MSU, I noticed a distinct lack of trivia in the grad student community. I decided to fix that by sending out a message to the grad students in my departments and creating a new team for anyone who wanted to join. I've made some of my closest friends at MSU through our trivia team and will even say that we're not half bad! We now look forward to trivia Tuesdays every week and have learned an assortment of fun facts across a range of topics. We just learned that there are actually regional and national bar trivia competitions through Sporcle and are formally registering our team so that we can compete-- maybe we'll make it big this fall!
