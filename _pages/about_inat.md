## iNaturalist

I am a huge fan of the iNaturalist community science platform and make sure to support it through my own volunteer efforts. As a result, I have been the lead organizer of the [iNaturalist City Nature Challenge](https://www.inaturalist.org/pages/citynaturechallenge) in Lansing for two years (2025 & 2026) and recently became an [iNaturalist ambassador](https://www.inaturalist.org/pages/ambassadors). The City Nature Challenge is an annual global bioblitz aimed at documenting wildlife in urban areas. As a local organizer, I scheduled and attended guided, taxon-focused nature walks led by local experts and advertised them to the community. I personally reached out to local experts on birds, plants, and insects to recruit walk leads and sourced funding to have coffee and donuts for participants as well as a prize for whoever made the most observations. The walks that I organized significantly increased local participation in the bioblitz, helped build connections with community members and organizations, and generated thousands of new data points to be used by scientists across the globe. Perhaps most importantly, they were super fun and informative! I hope to continue organizing future City Nature Challenges and other bioblitz events in the future. You can see our 2025 City Nature Challenge project [here](https://www.inaturalist.org/projects/city-nature-challenge-2025-greater-lansing-area) or our 2026 project [here](https://www.inaturalist.org/projects/city-nature-challenge-2026-greater-lansing-area).
