## MSU Bug House

When I'm not too busy with research, I occasionally volunteer at the MSU Bug House. The Bug House is a collection of both preserved and live specimens used to teach visitors about insects and arachnids.
Organizations like MSU classes or clubs can book tours of the Bug House, but it is also open to the public regularly. As a result, I have learned how to talk about bugs to both children and adults with
a wide range of comfort levels. I love volunteering at the Bug House because even visitors with reservations about bugs tend to be curious and open-minded, and I can often see them become more comfortable with
the specimens in real time (sometimes even enough to hold them!). I received an MSU Bug House fellowship for my volunteer efforts in 2025.
