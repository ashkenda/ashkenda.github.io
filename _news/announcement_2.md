I presented the first chapter of my research at the International Conference on Harmful Algae in Punta Arenas, Chile! This was my first talk at a conference, and I was so priviledged for it to take place in such an amazing location with a great community of people. This talk broke down some of the most important predictors of microcystin in U.S. lakes and delved into the relationship between microcystin and pH, which came out as most important. I got some really interesting comments and feedback from this talk which I will be taking into account when writing my paper. After the conference, I got to go backpacking through Torres del Paine National Park, which I think was probably the coolest place I've ever been.
