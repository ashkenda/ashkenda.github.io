I gave an oral presentation on the second chapter of my master's thesis at the ASLO-SIL Conference in Montreal! My talk was part of the session "Novel Ways to Improve Mechanistic Understanding and Prediction of Harmful Algal Blooms (Habs) Across Aquatic Ecosystems" and was titled _TOXIC RELATIONSHIPS: PREDICTING DRIVERS OF LAKE MICROCYSTIN ACROSS THE U.S. AND INTO THE FUTURE_. I talked about the importance of "ecological context" (which I defined as relatively unchanging landscape and morphometric variables) alongside climate change and land use variables when predicting microcystin under future climate scenarios. Every talk in the session was super cool and insightful.
